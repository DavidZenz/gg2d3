<objective>
Harden sf faceting validation so `facet_wrap()` and `facet_grid()` widgets containing point, line, polygon, mixed-family, and empty-panel cases prove panel-local counts and bbox/projection isolation.
</objective>

<threat_model>
## Threat Model

| Threat | Severity | Mitigation |
|--------|----------|------------|
| sf marks bleed into the wrong facet panel | high | Assert exact `.geom-sf` counts per panel for wrap and grid fixtures, including zero-count empty panels. |
| A global sf bbox distorts panel-local projection | high | Add IR assertions that non-empty panels have distinct finite `sf_bbox` values and empty panels have `NULL`; add browser assertions that rendered anchors are finite and within each panel. |
| Mixed sf families regress skipped-row or panel alignment | medium | Include mixed polygon/point/line fixtures with unsupported rows and assert only accepted rows render. |
| Browser validation becomes screenshot-dependent | low | Keep assertions DOM/IR-based and avoid screenshot diffs as the primary gate. |
</threat_model>
