# Roadmap: gg2d3

## Overview

This roadmap defines milestone **v1.5 Non-Cartesian Systems & Advanced Stats**. The phase structure continues from v1.4 and focuses on radial coordinate systems and deepening statistical support.

## Milestones

- ✅ **v1.0 MVP** — Phases 1-12 (shipped 2026-02-16)
- ✅ **v1.1 Interactive Exploration** — Phases 13-15 (shipped 2026-03-31)
- ✅ **v1.2 Smooth Transitions & Scale Parity** — Phases 16-17 (shipped 2026-03-31)
- ✅ **v1.3 Advanced Facets & Custom Interactivity** — Phases 18-19 (shipped 2026-03-31)
- ✅ **v1.4 Comprehensive Theme Parity & Reference Geoms** — Phases 20-21 (shipped 2026-03-31)
- 🟡 **v1.5 Non-Cartesian Systems & Advanced Stats** — Phases 22-23 (planned)

## Phases

### Phase 22 - Polar Coordinates

**Goal:** Implement `coord_polar` support for pie charts, coxcomb plots, and radar charts.

**Requirements:** COORD-03, COORD-04

**Success Criteria (observable):**
1. `coord_polar()` correctly transforms Cartesian aesthetics into radial/angular SVG elements.
2. Circular and radial grid lines and labels are correctly rendered.

### Phase 23 - Advanced Statistical Geoms

**Goal:** Deepen support for statistical layers that require kernel estimation or complex smoothing.

**Requirements:** GEOM-18, GEOM-19

**Success Criteria (observable):**
1. `geom_density` renders correctly with accurate area fill.
2. `geom_smooth` supports loess and gam methods by utilizing pre-computed paths from R.
