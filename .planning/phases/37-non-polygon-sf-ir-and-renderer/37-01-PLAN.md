<objective>
Expand the R-side `geom_sf()` IR contract so point-family and line-family geometries are accepted, classified, serialized, diagnosed, and included in panel `sf_bbox` metadata without regressing existing polygon behavior.

This plan creates the data contract that later renderer and browser plans consume.
</objective>

<threat_model>
## Threat Model

| Threat | Severity | Mitigation |
|--------|----------|------------|
| Renderer-private geometry metadata leaks into public callback rows | medium | Keep new family fields diagnostic/contract-oriented and preserve later payload sanitization checks for `_geom`, `_centroid`, and child geometry fields. |
| Malformed sf rows bypass skipped-row diagnostics | medium | Preserve missing/empty/invalid/unsupported filtering and extend tests for unsupported `GEOMETRYCOLLECTION`. |
| Mixed geometry support breaks source row identity | high | Tests must assert `row_id`, `accepted_rows`, `skipped_rows`, and parallel `data`/`geometries` arrays for accepted point, line, polygon, and mixed rows. |
</threat_model>
