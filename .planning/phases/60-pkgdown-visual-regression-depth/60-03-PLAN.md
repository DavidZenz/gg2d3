    - **CI behavior:** runs inside `pkgdown.yaml` after the build; uploads a `pkgdown-visual-<run_id>` artifact; in CI (`GG2D3_BROWSER_VISUAL_CI=true`) a skip escalates to a failure; a missing runner Chrome degrades to a non-fatal skip (D-03).
